# Design assets

## Welcome photograph

- Asset: `assets/images/welcome_road.png` (1024 × 1536 PNG).
- Created: 2026-09-21 using the built-in `image_gen` tool through the imagegen skill.
- This is an AI-generated generic scenic road photograph, made to match the composition of the welcome screen supplied by the user. It is not a photograph of a monitored road or evidence of local road conditions.
- Branding, text, contrast overlays, and controls are rendered separately by Flutter.
- The original generated image was copied into the project without image edits.

Final generation prompt:

```text
Use case: photorealistic-natural
Asset type: Portrait welcome-screen photographic background for the RoadGuard traveler mobile app, 1024x1536 pixels or a similar tall portrait aspect ratio.
Primary request: A realistic scenic two-lane asphalt road with a yellow dashed center line, running straight from the bottom center of the frame toward a distant mountain range. Green trees and low roadside vegetation flank the road. Generic landscape, not a specific real location.
Composition/framing: Centered symmetrical road perspective. Road vanishing point and mountain horizon around 60 percent of the image height, leaving a large open clear deep blue sky across the upper half for white app branding that will be rendered separately in Flutter. The road occupies most of the lower third. Mountains remain modest in height. Natural camera photography, detailed asphalt texture, believable road markings and perspective.
Lighting/mood: Bright clean daylight, calm and welcoming, rich navy-to-blue sky, fresh green vegetation, gently sunlit mountains. Keep the lower road relatively dark so white interface text can be overlaid.
Constraints: Produce only the photograph. Absolutely no text, lettering, logos, UI, phone frame, watermark, people, vehicles, buildings, signboards, or icons. No composited typography. No collage. No illustrated or cartoon treatment.
```
